# Campeonato do Bairro — privacy

The app has no advertising, tracking, analytics, purchases, or developer-operated server. Tournament names, teams, rosters, scores, notes, polls and local settings are stored on the device. You can use the tournament tools without an account or network connection.

Choose a file to open, or send a copy directly to someone. Changes stay on this device until you share again. Invite trusted co-organizers with editing permission; other invitees should have read-only access. Shared data includes team and player names, reports, votes, flags and pseudonymous author identifiers. Do not enter sensitive information. There is no public directory, chat, or feed.

Choose a file to open, or send a copy directly to someone. Changes stay on this device until you share again. No email address is requested. Polls are informal community voting, not identity verification.

Notifications are local and optional. The app asks only after the user enables reminders. Permission denial does not prevent tournament use. Widgets read an on-device snapshot through the app's own App Group. Widgets do not request network data. The app does not use the camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, HealthKit or ATT.

Owners can approve/reject submitted reports, inspect flags, block ballots and pending reports, and revoke invitations using Apple's sharing sheet. No claim of adversarial access control or public social-network moderation is made.

Network failures preserve the data and show an error; retry deletion after restoring access.
